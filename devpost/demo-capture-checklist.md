# Demo Video Capture Checklist

Target length: 90–150 seconds, within Devpost's 1–3 minute requirement.

## Before recording

- Use a clean demo profile and demo-only conversation data.
- Confirm the deployed app opens on the phone and voice, transcript saving, dashboard updates, and chat history work.
- Enter the app before recording so the shared password is not visible.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb and hide notifications, API keys, and personal information.
- Record vertically, with the app filling the screen and assistant audio clear.

## Capture sequence

- [ ] Open Chats and start a new voice conversation about one goal, one real-life constraint, and one next step.
- [ ] Show the live transcript and the assistant's response.
- [ ] End and save the conversation; show its saved summary in the chat.
- [ ] Open Life Dashboard; show the goal, future target, ordered steps, and progress.
- [ ] Mark one task complete and show that it remains visible while progress updates.
- [ ] Start a second chat and ask a follow-up that needs the existing goal context.
- [ ] Return to the first chat or dashboard to show the plan and conversation history are still there.

## Review before sharing

- [ ] The finished video is 1–3 minutes and demonstrates the app working end to end.
- [ ] Speech and assistant audio are understandable; the transcript and dashboard text are readable.
- [ ] No password, credential, notification, or private conversation appears.
- [ ] Every shown action happened in the app during capture; no edit implies behavior that was not demonstrated.
- [ ] The uploaded video is viewable by reviewers without signing in, and its link is ready for Devpost.
